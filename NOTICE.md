- the textures and figure cards cut from them: `assets/cliff.jpg`, `assets/rock.jpg`, `assets/slope.jpg`, `assets/leaf0.png`, `assets/leaf1.png`, `assets/leaf2.png`, `assets/1265_figures.webp`, `assets/1266_figures.webp`, `assets/1267_cranes.webp`, `assets/1267_elders.webp`, `assets/1267_kids.webp`

- in `docs/media/`: the photograph halves of `pair-*.jpg`, the photograph layers of `overlay-s08.jpg`, the opening frames of `hero.webp` (the painting before it dissolves into the 3D view), the photograph thumbnails in `seams.png`, and the painting overlay and card picture in `interface.jpg`
- the same textures wherever they show inside a screenshot of the game

If you reuse the code or the screenshots, leave these images out or get permission for them yourself.
