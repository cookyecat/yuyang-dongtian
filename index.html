怎么玩

自由镜头

  • WASD 或方向键移动，按住鼠标拖动转向
  • Q / E 下降、上升，Shift 加速，滚轮调速度
  • Tab 打开游线图，V 飞到最近景点的画境视角，可拖滑块对照原画
  • T 拨到下一个时辰（晨、午、昏、夜），右上角时辰按钮可细调；一日 24 分钟

人物

  • 点人物：镜头推近，看介绍
  • F 接管：WASD 行走，拖动绕看，滚轮远近，点地面自动走过去
  • 走到别人身边按 E：交谈、作揖、同行、邀坐
  • 点水面投石，走近仙鹤会惊飞
  • 夜里各殿亭檐下点起灯笼；接管的人手里提一盏灯
  • Esc 一层层退回

作者与题名壁

  • 角落的朱印「李揚」：点开看跋，作者与 GitHub、X
  • 卷尾四陽洞外，路钻进一道窄峡通向画外，峡口北壁上有作者题刻
  • 玉女潭玉光閣西边路旁的石屏是题名壁，点一下或走近按 E 就能留名
  • 峡口坐着一位写生的画师，接管游人走过去可以和他聊几句

手机

  • 左下按住出摇杆，右侧拖动转向，双指上下拖动升降

中英文

  • 右上角 EN 按钮切换到英文界面；链接加 ?lang=en 直接打开英文版

How to Wander

Free camera

  • WASD or the arrow keys to move; hold the mouse button and drag to turn
  • Q / E to sink and rise, Shift to go faster, the mouse wheel to set your speed
  • Tab opens the route map. V flies to the painting view of the nearest scene, the angle the painter chose, where a slider fades in a photograph of the painting
  • T moves on to the next time of day (morning, noon, dusk, night); the hour button at top right fine-tunes it. A whole day lasts 24 minutes

Figures

  • Click a figure: the camera draws near and their story appears
  • F to take their place: WASD to walk, drag to look around, the wheel to zoom, click the ground to walk there
  • Walk up to someone and press E to talk, bow, walk together or sit together
  • Click the water to toss in a pebble; come too close to the cranes and they take flight
  • At night lanterns glow under the eaves, and the figure you play carries a lamp
  • Esc steps back, one layer at a time

The makers and the inscription wall

  • The red seal 李揚 in the corner opens the colophon, with links to the makers
  • Beyond Siyang Cave, where the scroll ends, the path slips into a narrow gorge and out of the painting; the makers’ inscription is carved on the rock wall at its mouth
  • The stone screen beside the path west of the Jade Light Tower, at the Jade Maiden Pool, is the inscription wall: click it, or walk up and press E, to leave your name
  • A painter sits sketching at the gorge mouth; walk a figure over to him for a few words

On a phone

  • Touch and hold at lower left for a joystick, drag on the right to turn, drag two fingers up or down to rise or sink

Language

  • The 中文 button at top right switches to Chinese. Add ?lang=en to the address to open the English version directly

跋Colophon

《玉阳洞天图卷》旧传明代仇英所作，今藏天津博物馆。卷中金晶巖、大觀廊、玉女潭、超然亭、龍湫、四陽洞，自右而左一路展开，移步换景。

我们以展厅里拍下的十三帧为底本，在浏览器里一山一石、一屋一人地把它搭成可以走进去的青绿山水：可行，可望，可游，可居。原画接缝处看不到的地方，是按画意补出来的；哪里不像，欢迎指正。

The handscroll Yuyang Dongtian, the Grotto-Heaven of Yuyang, has long been attributed to the Ming painter Qiu Ying and is now in the Tianjin Museum. Golden Crystal Cliff, the Daguan Gallery, the Jade Maiden Pool, the Chaoran Pavilion, the Dragon Pool and Siyang Cave unfold along it from right to left, the scenery changing with every step.

Working from thirteen photographs taken in the gallery, we rebuilt it in the browser, mountain by mountain and stone by stone, house by house and figure by figure, as a blue-and-green landscape you can walk into: one to travel through, to gaze upon, to wander in and to dwell in. Where the seams between the photographs hid the painting, we filled the gaps in its spirit. If anything looks wrong, we would be glad to hear.

题名壁Inscription Wall

古人游山，爱在石壁上留名。走到这里的朋友，也请留下名字：会刻在玉女潭边的石屏上，后来的游人都看得到。点开石壁上的名字，能看到题名人留下的社交账号。Travellers in old China loved to leave their names on the rocks of the mountains they climbed. You who have come this far, leave yours too: it will be cut into the stone screen by the Jade Maiden Pool, for every later visitor to see. Open a name on the wall to find the social account its owner left.

石壁上只刻名字，别人点开你的名字才看得到账号。带账号的题名要等主人过目，通过后才刻上。Only your name is carved on the stone; your account shows when someone opens your name. Entries with an account are carved once the host has looked them over.

名字和留言里请勿留电话、网址；想让人找到你，填社交账号就好。不雅的字句会被拦下。Please keep phone numbers and web addresses out of your name and line; to let people find you, add a social account instead. Improper words will be turned away. 看跋Read the colophon · 去玉女潭看题名壁See the wall at the Jade Maiden Pool
